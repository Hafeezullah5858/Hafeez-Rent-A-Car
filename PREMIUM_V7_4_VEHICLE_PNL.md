# Premium V7.4 — Vehicle P&L & Expense Breakdown

Adds a vehicle-specific profit & loss view on top of V7.3 fleet comparison.

## Features
- Select any vehicle and Daily/Weekly/Monthly/Custom period.
- Income: rental billed, rental collected, other income, total income.
- Expenses: fuel, maintenance, other ledger expenses, total expenses.
- Net result and outstanding balance.
- Monthly trend rows across the selected period.
- Download a vehicle P&L CSV.
- Print/PDF uses the existing branded vehicle report pipeline.

No new runtime dependency was introduced.
