# Ride Accounting

## Ride sources
- Yango
- inDrive
- Offline
- Direct Booking

## Service types
- Local Ride
- City-to-City

## Income
Fare is recorded as Ride Income.

## Expenses
Platform commission, fuel, toll/motorway/parking, driver expense/share and other ride expense are recorded as Ride Expense.

## Profit
Net Ride Profit = Fare - Platform Commission - Fuel - Toll/Parking - Driver Expense - Other Expense.

## Offline behavior
Ride records are stored locally and can be entered without internet. This is offline data entry, not live Yango/inDrive synchronization.

## Reports
Linked ride entries appear in the normal financial reports and are included in PDF/XLSX exports.
